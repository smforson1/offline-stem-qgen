Why ScanQ

Built for the Classroom,
Not the Cloud

Every design decision was made with Ghanaian JHS/SHS teachers in mind — where internet is unreliable, devices are limited, and time is precious.

📵
Fully Offline
The entire pipeline — OCR, AI, database, PDF export — runs on your local network. Zero internet dependency after setup.
⚡
Fast Generation
Questions stream back in real time via Server-Sent Events. Typically under 15 seconds for a full set on a modern laptop CPU.
📷
Camera or Gallery
Photograph a textbook page directly, or upload an existing photo from your gallery. PaddleOCR handles the text extraction.
🎯
Fully Configurable
Choose subject, difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard), question format (MCQ or Short Answer), and question count before every session.
📊
Score & Review
Answer your questions in-app, get instant scoring, and review every question with correct answer explanations.
📄
PDF Export
Export any session as a formatted exam worksheet with ReportLab. Share via any Android share sheet — WhatsApp, email, print.

The Pipeline

From Textbook Page
to Practice Quiz

Three steps, all running on your local Wi-Fi network. No data leaves your setup.

📷

1. Capture

Photograph or upload a textbook page. Configure your subject, difficulty, format and question count on the pre-flight settings sheet.

PaddleOCR · Image Preprocessing
🧠

2. Generate

The Flask backend extracts text via OCR, builds a structured prompt, and runs the quantised LLM. Questions stream back in real time as they are produced.

Qwen2.5-0.5B GGUF · llama.cpp
✅

3. Practice & Export

Answer questions in-app, see your score, review every answer with explanations, and export a formatted PDF worksheet in one tap.

SQLite · ReportLab PDF
